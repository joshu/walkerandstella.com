Walker and Stella

Privacy

The Adventures of Walker and Stella collects no personal information. None.

What the game collects
Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game does not send anything about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.
What stays on your device
Your progress (levels finished, stars, kibble, outfits, photos found) and a few settings are saved on your iPhone or iPad. They never leave it, except in your device's own backups, which Apple handles. Deleting the game deletes them.
Purchases
Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The game only learns which items your account owns, so it can unlock them.
Children
Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.
This website
This site uses no cookies and no analytics. The company hosting it may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as all web hosts do.
