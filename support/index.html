Walker and Stella

Support

Stuck, or something not working? Write to hello@walkerandstella.com. A real person reads every message.

I bought the full story on another device. How do I get it here?
Open the Treat jar tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.
I'm stuck on a level.
Tap the light bulb for a hint: a ghost of the dogs shows the next part of the way through. Each step costs one Charlie Bear the first time and is free after that. You earn Charlie Bears by finding bully sticks and finishing eras. You can also skip a level for two Charlie Bears.
How do I play?
Tap the ? button in the scrapbook or in any level. It shows the buttons, each era's goal, and what everything in the rooms does.
I deleted the game and my progress is gone.
Progress is kept on the device, so deleting the game deletes it. Purchases are not lost: reinstall, then use Restore Purchases.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, or write to us and we'll help.
