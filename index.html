The Adventures of
Walker and Stella

This is the story of how two dogs from a garage ended up on a couch.

Walker was born in Ireland and sent to a kennel in America, where thirty-three Tibetan terriers lived in crates in a garage. Then a girl with a bitten ear decided he needed looking after. Together they got out, made it through the shelter, and found a home that had no idea what it was in for.

You play the dogs. Walker jumps. Stella holds her ground. Get them both through each room, one era of their story at a time.

What's in it

  • Two dogs, one puzzle. Switch between them: Walker climbs on Stella, Stella stands guard so Walker can get past.
  • Nine eras of a true story, from a flight across the Atlantic to the holiday photo.
  • A scrapbook that fills up as you find the bully sticks hidden in each room.
  • A groomer for haircuts and outfits, paid for in kibble you earn by playing.
  • Hints when you're stuck: a ghost of the dogs shows you the next step.
  • Free to start. The first three eras are free. One purchase opens the rest of the story. No ads, no tracking, nothing collected.

Based on real dogs

Walker and Stella are real, and so is most of what happens to them. The people's names have been changed. The dogs would like it known that they come out of it very well.
